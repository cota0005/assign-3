Cons' Top Artists and their impact

I grew up surronded by music thanks to my dad. From rock to industrial to nu-metal to jazz to folklore. And as I grew up I began also discovering new music every day. My dad was a huge influence, since I was a kid I would open a closet full of CDs and DVDs, and he would burn CDs with music that he knew my mom or I would like. There wans't a moment in the car where music wasnt playing. Music has always been an essential part of my life, there's no day that I go without listening to music somehow.

This list consists of artists that have impacted my life in different ways. Some of this artists Ive been listening to since I was a kid, and others Ive just recently discovered.

List of Top Artists:

  1. Nine Inch Nails
  2. David Bowie
  3. Milo J
  4. Limp Bizkit
  5. Social Distortion
  6. Death Cab for Cutie
  7. Jamiroquai

Nine Inch Nails

Nine Inch Nails will always be in my top 1. I discovered this band when I was 13, watching a documentary on how Hurt and the rest of the downward spiral album was produced with my dad. At the end of said documentary, they played the song of "Hurt" from start to finish, As I was listening tears began to fall, which it is hilarious because how could a 13 year old connect with such a heavy song like "Hurt"?

Besides of how the song impacted me, I was also amazed by how the whole album itself was produced and how Trent Reznor was able to create such a good album, even though it was their second album.

To all this, here are some of my favourite songs:

  • Heresy
  • The Fragile
  • Sin
  • La Mer
  • The Downward Spiral

And a special mention to "Im Afraid of Americans" a song by David Bowie that Nine Inch Nails remixed and the music video is my favourite piece of audiovisual media

David Bowie

Every morning on my way to school, when I was in forth grade, my dad would put his USB drive and play his playlists, and every morning I would ask for the song "Starman".

Since then I've always been inspired by the way he would change his looks for every album, how he was always loyal to his opinions and values, and how he could easily influence people through his music.

Fun fact: "Life on Mars" was written to Frank Sinatra, and it's about meeting your hero and how horrible it could go.

Milo J

Now this artist is way more recent. Milo J is an Argentine singer and songwriter, he is barely 19 years old and has already created a huge impact in the Latinamerican culture.

He started by producing trap music, but as he realized that he wanted to move on from that side, he decided to produce a folklore album called "La vida era mas corta".
