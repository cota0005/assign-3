    "La Jangada", Milo J uniting artists from different generations

    On the past 30th of September, Milo J realesed a feature film where he presented his whole album live with 35 muscians and artists in the middle of a land in Santiago del Estero. In between those artists that collaborated was: Trueno, bringingthe streets of argentina and the love for his home with the song "Gil", Soledad Pastorutti in "Lucía", Cuti and Roberto Carbajal in "El Invisible", Lázaro Caballero, a young new promise that had never presented humself before, in "Radamel" and Mon Laferte interpreting the part of Mercedes Sosa in "Jangadero"

    Nine Inch Noize finally releases physical project album

    Nine Inch Nails in collaboration with Boys Noize, has finally realeased a physical album, consisting on reworked NIN and Destroy Angels tracks.
