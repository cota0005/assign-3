Nine Inch Nails

Nine Inch Nails will always be in my top 1. I discovered this band when I was 13, watching a documentary on how "Hurt" and the rest of The Downward Spiral album were produced with my dad.

Having such an interesting way to experiment and create music, the band impacted me through the way Trent Reznor, the main singer, composer, and producer, would take such complex concepts and translate them into his lyrics.

So, here's a list of some of my favourite songs:

  • Heresy
  • The Fragile
  • Sin
  • La Mer
  • The Downward Spiral

And a special mention to I'm Afraid of Americans, a song by David Bowie that Nine Inch Nails remixed. The music video is one of my favourite pieces of audiovisual media.

David Bowie

Every morning on my way to school, when I was in fourth grade, my dad would put his USB drive in and play his playlists. Every morning I would ask for the song "Starman."

Since then, I've always been inspired by the way he would change his looks for every album, how he was always loyal to his opinions and values, and how he could easily influence people through his music.

Fun fact: Life on Mars? was written from an idea connected to Frank Sinatra's "My Way."

Milo J

Now this artist is way newer. Milo J is an Argentine singer and songwriter. He is still very young and has already created a huge impact in Latin American music.

He started by producing trap music, but as he realized that he wanted to move on from that side, he decided to produce a folklore-inspired album called La vida era más corta.

"Pensaste que la vida era más corta como para cumplir tu promesa de amarnos para siempre."

This was the line that stuck with Milo J and helped inspire the album La vida era más corta.
